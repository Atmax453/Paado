GOOD TO KNOW

Island
info.

When and where?

Saturday 28 November, from 12PM, at Paado Island in Winneba. Free transport leaves from Sir Charles Beach.

How much are tickets?

Single is ₵100 for one person. Double is ₵180 for two. Both include transport, a canoe ride, a cup for drinks, all games, music and DJ sessions. Buy online with MoMo or card, or dial *713*33*347#.

How do I get to the island?

Free transport leaves from Sir Charles Beach and is included with your ticket.

I paid online. Where is my ticket?

Your ticket code shows as soon as payment goes through. Lost it? Tap "Find my ticket" and enter the phone number you used.

What should I wear?

Beachwear you don't mind getting wet. Bring a change of clothes and sunscreen.

Food and drinks?

Vendors will be on the island. Bring cash or mobile money.
